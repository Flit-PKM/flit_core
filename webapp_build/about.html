About Flit

Simple notes. Effective when you need them. Yours.

What is Flit?

Flit is a private place for your notes. Write them down, organize them, and find them later — without extra tools getting in the way.

Why Flit

Your notes should be easy to keep and fully under your control. Flit is built to stay useful: capture, organize, and sync — privately.

  • Flit Web — Notes in the browser.
  • Flit-App — Capture notes and ideas on the go.
  • Still — Meditation timer and journaling to track sessions, set intentions, and reflect.

What you get

  • Notes, categories, and links
  • Markdown import and export
  • Sync and backup on Flit Core
  • MCP access and connected apps
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
