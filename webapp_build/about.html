About Flit-PKM

Your Private Second Brain — Secure, Connected, Enhanced.

What is Flit?

Flit is your modern Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) ecosystem — a private, secure space where your notes, insights, and daily reflections come together with powerful AI to help you think deeper and discover more.
Imagine your thoughts no longer scattered: Flit blends effortless note-taking with smart AI that summarizes, connects ideas, and uncovers hidden patterns and fresh insights you might never have spotted on your own. It's built for people who want their knowledge to feel alive, organized, and truly theirs.

Our Mission

We believe your knowledge should be connected, effortless to navigate, and completely under your control. Flit empowers you to build a growing personal knowledge base with intuitive tools for linking ideas, organizing thoughts, and revealing meaningful insights — all while keeping everything 100% private.
Our connected apps make it even better:

  • Flit-App — Capture notes and ideas anytime, anywhere.
  • Still — Elegant meditation timer and journaling to track sessions, set intentions, and reflect deeply.
  • And more on the way soon...

Together, they bridge the different parts of your life — work, wellness, creativity — into one unified, private second brain that evolves with you.
